Wood-Fired Pizza Making

Join Chef Marco in a fun and interactive culinary experience. Learn authentic Neapolitan dough fermentation, fresh sauce preparation, and traditional wood-fired baking techniques using premium Italian ingredients.

  • Location: Bella Italia Kitchen, Olaya Street
  • Chef name: Chef Marco Rossi
  • Cuisine type: Authentic Italian
  • Workshop type: Interactive Cooking Class

Cost

220 SAR

What to Expect

  • Complete hands-on pizza making practice with individual prep stations.
  • All fresh ingredients, aprons, and utensils provided.
  • Tasting session of your freshly baked pizza with complimentary beverages at the end.
  • Certificate of participation signed by Chef Marco.

Requirements

  • Please arrive 15 minutes prior to session start time.
