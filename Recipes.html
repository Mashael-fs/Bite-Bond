Main Dish

Chicken Kabsa

Ingredients:

  • Chicken - 1 kg
  • Basmati Rice - 2 cups
  • Onion & Tomato - 1 each
  • Kabsa Spices - 1 tbsp

Instructions:

  1. Sauté onion, add chicken and spices, then add water and cook until tender.
  2. Add rice to the broth and simmer until fully cooked.
60m Medium 5
Appetizers

Assorted Nigiri Sushi Platter

Ingredients:

  • Sushi Rice - 2 cups
  • Sushi Vinegar - 3 tbsp
  • Fresh Fish Slices (Salmon/Tuna) - 200g
  • Wasabi & Soy Sauce - to taste

Instructions:

  1. Season cooked rice with vinegar and let cool.
  2. Shape rice into small mounds and top with sliced fish.
45m Hard 2
Dessert

Decadent Chocolate Fudge Cake

Ingredients:

  • Flour & Sugar - 2 cups each
  • Cocoa Powder - 3/4 cup
  • Eggs - 2
  • Butter & Chocolate - 1 cup each

Instructions:

  1. Mix dry and wet ingredients, then bake at 175°C for 30-35 mins.
  2. Melt chocolate with butter to make fudge frosting and coat the cooled cake.
2h Medium 8
