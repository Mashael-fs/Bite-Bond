Knife Skills

A safe, steady chopping technique

Use a stable board and a claw grip for confident, even chopping.

Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Place a damp cloth under the board.
  2. Hold the handle firmly and keep fingertips curled.
  3. Guide the blade with your knuckles.
  4. Cut slowly into evenly sized pieces.
